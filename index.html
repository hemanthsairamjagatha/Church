FIRST-TIME GUEST GUIDE

Plan Your Visit to Grace Haven

We can't wait to meet you! Here is what you can look forward to during your Sunday visit:

1

Reserved Guest Parking

Follow our signs to the front lot where spaces are specially reserved for first-time visitors. Our parking attendants are happy to assist.

2

Warm Welcome & Refreshments

Stop by the Welcome Lounge in the main foyer for complimentary coffee, tea, and warm pastries before the service begins.

3

Safe & Engaging Kids Programs

Children aged 0–11 can check in safely at our Grace Kids center where caring, background-checked teachers will guide their lesson.

4

Come as You Are

Dress comfortably. From jeans and t-shirts to Sunday best, everyone is embraced just as they are.

BIOGRAPHY

The Journey & Heart of Pastor Ezekiel

“For every way that someone can be lost, we want to provide a practical way for them to be found in the very place that they have ended up.”

A Calling of Faith & Purpose

Ezekiel.G is the Senior Pastor of Rock Church. A former NFL defensive back for the San Diego Chargers, Ezekiel experienced a life-changing encounter with Jesus Christ that led him into youth ministry and pastoral leadership.

Vision & Ministry

Under Pastor Ezekiel’s leadership, the church focuses on equipping believers to Know God, Grow in Community, and Make a Difference through local outreach and global impact.
